# Leave Policy (sample, fictional)

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, and new joiners get a pro-rated amount for their first year.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Any balance above 5 days expires on 31 March.

Sick leave is 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

Leave of more than 3 days must be requested in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager.
