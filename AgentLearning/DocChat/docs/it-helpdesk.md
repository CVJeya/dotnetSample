# IT Helpdesk Guide (sample, fictional)

Passwords expire every 90 days. You can reset yours at any time using the self-service portal; you do not need to raise a ticket.

A VPN connection is required to reach the staging environment from outside the office. Install the VPN client from the software portal and sign in with your work account.

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the helpdesk within 24 hours so it can be remotely locked.

For anything else, email the helpdesk at helpdesk@example.com. Urgent outages are handled first.
