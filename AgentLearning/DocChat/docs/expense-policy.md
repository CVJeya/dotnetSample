# Travel and Expense Policy (sample, fictional)

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with original receipts attached. Claims older than 30 days need director approval.

Any single expense above INR 10,000 needs prior written approval from your manager.

Flights must be economy class and booked through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Hotel stays are capped at INR 8,000 per night.
